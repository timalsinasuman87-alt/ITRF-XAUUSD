# ITRF v0.10.6 Continuation-Value Results

## Status

The pre-registered v0.10.6 continuation-value policy has been evaluated on the
complete locked January 2025-April 2026 external XAU/USD sample. The model used
only causal start-of-bar state, transported v0.10.3 dynamic hazards, and the R
available by exiting at the current open. It exited at the earliest holding bar
where predicted continuation advantage was below zero.

The policy **failed** its pre-registered exploratory decision rule. It is
rejected and must not be rescued by changing the ridge penalty, adding a safety
margin, delaying activation, selecting a direction, or choosing a subgroup from
these results.

This historical segment had already been inspected in v0.10.4 and v0.10.5. The
result is an exploratory decision-model screen, not untouched validation or a
profitability claim. No data was downloaded, no live feed or broker connection
was used, no order was placed, and the incremental cost was $0.

## Paired uncertainty

- Mean managed-minus-baseline improvement: -0.05262R per event
- Circular block-bootstrap 95% interval: [-0.13623R, +0.03011R]

The point estimate is adverse and the interval includes zero. The policy also
had lower profit factors than baseline at both locked cost levels and worse
maximum drawdowns at both cost levels.

## Pre-registered decision

The policy failed all four required conditions:

1. mean paired improvement was not positive;
2. the bootstrap lower bound was not above zero;
3. managed profit factor did not exceed baseline at 0.05R or 0.10R; and
4. managed maximum drawdown was worse at 0.05R and 0.10R.

No condition may be relaxed after inspection.

## Research interpretation

v0.10.6 addressed the main conceptual weakness of v0.10.5 by learning the
complete realized continuation payoff rather than comparing only next-bar stop
and target hazards. That change was insufficient. The fixed linear value model
still classified 88.91% of events for early exit, mostly at the first eligible
bar, and surrendered 0.05262R per event relative to the unchanged path.

The evidence now distinguishes two statements: prior-path state can improve
next-bar event calibration, but neither the frozen myopic hazard action nor the
frozen linear continuation-value action converts that information into a
management edge. This is a useful negative result. It blocks threshold tuning
and prevents a calibration result from being mistaken for a trading strategy.

## Next research requirement

Do not tune this policy on the inspected external sample. Preserve v0.8 and the
v0.10 clean event core, quarantine both v0.10.5 and v0.10.6 management rules,
and stop management-policy iteration on this history.

The next defensible work is an architecture review of the entry mechanism and
its economic information set, followed by one pre-registered hypothesis that
adds genuinely new causal information. Any candidate must be frozen before a
new chronological XAU/USD period is observed. The required future holdout
remains untouched and mandatory before any paper or live-trading discussion.
