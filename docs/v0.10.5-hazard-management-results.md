# ITRF v0.10.5 Hazard-Management Results

## Status

The pre-registered v0.10.5 defensive-exit policy has been evaluated on the
complete locked January 2025-April 2026 external XAU/USD sample.  The rule used
the frozen v0.10.3 dynamic next-bar probabilities and exited at the earliest
post-entry bar open where `prob_stop > 3 * prob_target`.

The policy **failed** its pre-registered exploratory decision rule.  It is
rejected and must not be rescued by changing the probability multiplier,
selecting a direction, delaying activation, or choosing a subgroup from these
results.

The sample had already been inspected for v0.10.4 calibration.  This is an
exploratory policy screen, not untouched validation or a profitability claim.
No data was downloaded, no live feed or broker connection was used, no order
was placed, and the incremental cost was $0.

## Pairing and integrity

- Eligible unambiguous paired events: 1,046
- Unique paired event identifiers: 1,046
- Duplicate events: 0
- Defensive exits: 908 (86.81%)
- Unchanged baseline exits: 138
- Earliest/latest action holding bar: 2/32
- Mean action holding bar: 2.35
- Median action holding bar: 2
- Actions after a baseline terminal bar: 0
- Non-action managed outcomes exactly equal their baselines
- Maximum paired-improvement arithmetic error: 4.44e-16
- All outcomes were finite

The action therefore behaved as a very broad early-exit rule, despite its
payoff-derived boundary.  No additional signals were admitted after an early
exit, so opportunity-count changes do not explain the comparison.

## Paired uncertainty

- Mean managed-minus-baseline improvement: -0.06136R per event
- Circular block-bootstrap 95% interval: [-0.14535R, +0.02083R]

The point estimate is adverse and the interval includes zero.  The policy also
had lower profit factors than baseline at both locked cost levels and worse
maximum drawdowns at both cost levels.

## Pre-registered decision

The policy failed all four required conditions:

1. mean paired improvement was not positive;
2. the bootstrap lower bound was not above zero;
3. managed profit factor did not exceed baseline at 0.05R or 0.10R; and
4. managed maximum drawdown was worse at 0.05R and 0.10R.

No condition may be relaxed after inspection.

## Research interpretation

v0.10.4 established that completed prior-bar path state improves next-bar
hazard calibration.  v0.10.5 establishes that accurate hazard ranking does not
automatically create a valuable exit action.  The myopic terminal-pressure
comparison ignores the continuation value of surviving `NONE` bars and exits
86.81% of events, usually before their paths can develop.

That explanation is a mechanism interpretation, not permission to tune a
larger multiplier or later activation bar.  The exact v0.10.5 rule is
quarantined.

## Next research requirement

Do not search probability cutoffs on this sample.  A defensible next module
would first specify a full continuation-value or state-transition framework
that values `NONE`, future stop/target hazards, remaining holding time, and
transaction costs.  It may be developed only as a diagnostic on inspected
data, then frozen before any new chronological validation.  The required
post-2026-08-07 XAU/USD holdout remains untouched and mandatory before any
management rule can be promoted.
