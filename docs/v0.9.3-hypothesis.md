# ITRF v0.9.3 Pre-Registered Sequence-Flow Hypothesis

Status: pre-registered before calculating sequence-flow outcomes.

## Purpose

v0.9.2 rejected the idea that the sign of CME Gold aggressor volume delta on
the confirmation bar alone validates a v0.9.1 XAU/USD signal. v0.9.3 tests a
different economic mechanism: whether aggressive flow reverses over the full
causal sequence from the liquidity sweep through structure confirmation.

The research and potential traded instrument remains XAU/USD. Historical CME
Gold futures trades are validation context only. No live data, futures trading,
broker connection, or parameter optimization is permitted.

## Frozen sequence definition

- Keep every v0.9.1 arm, replacement, invalidation, expiry, structure,
  displacement, and eight-bar confirmation rule unchanged.
- Join futures order-flow bars to XAU/USD by exact UTC 15-minute bar-start
  timestamps only.
- For each completed v0.9.1 confirmation, use the inclusive sequence beginning
  at `v091_source_sweep_time` and ending at the confirmation timestamp.
- Require a valid exact futures-flow match for every XAU/USD bar in that
  sequence. Missing flow fails closed; nearest-time filling is prohibited.
- Sequence buy volume is the sum of `B`-side trade size across the sequence.
- Sequence sell volume is the sum of `A`-side trade size across the sequence.
- Sequence delta is sequence buy volume minus sequence sell volume.
- Sequence signed-volume ratio is sequence delta divided by sequence buy plus
  sell volume. Unknown-side volume is reported but excluded from this ratio.

## Primary gate

- A v0.9.1 long passes only when sequence delta is positive.
- A v0.9.1 short passes only when sequence delta is negative.
- Zero delta, zero specified volume, or incomplete sequence coverage fails.
- No delta magnitude, ratio threshold, session, direction, or sequence age may
  be selected from the development result.

## Evaluation

The unchanged 32-bar XAU/USD forward outcome is used. Non-overlap is calculated
separately for all same-period v0.9.1 confirmations and for v0.9.3 accepted
confirmations. Report direction, sample count, average R, hit rates, stop rate,
chronological quarters, and fixed 0.05R/0.10R cost diagnostics.

The May 1 through August 7, 2026 interval is development data. A positive
result cannot establish profitability or justify deployment. Promotion still
requires a future untouched period after August 7 with at least 100
non-overlapping observations and at least three calendar months.
