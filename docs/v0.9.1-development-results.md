# ITRF v0.9.1 development results

## Status

The causal state machine is technically valid but did not reveal a stable
strategic edge on the inspected development feeds. No parameter was optimized
and no cohort is promoted to a production rule.

## Databento CME Gold development feed

The state machine produced 1,842 long arms, 2,277 short arms, 1,623
replacements, 1,416 invalidations, 874 expirations, and 206 confirmations. One
confirmation was too near the end for a complete 32-bar forward label, leaving
205 stored observations and 179 non-overlapping candidates.

| Direction | Non-overlapping samples | Average R | Stopped |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Long | 90 | -0.056 | 68.89% |
| Short | 89 | -0.281 | 78.65% |

The four chronological non-overlapping development quarters averaged -0.267R,
-0.067R, -0.091R, and -0.244R. This is consistently negative rather than a
single-period failure.

## HistData XAUUSD development feed

The same frozen rules produced 35 complete observations and 30 non-overlapping
candidates.

| Direction | Non-overlapping samples | Average R | Stopped |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Long | 22 | +0.045 | 68.18% |
| Short | 8 | -0.500 | 87.50% |

The sample is too small and the four chronological development quarters were
mixed. It does not validate the Databento result or establish an edge.

## Regime diagnostic

Long confirmations while EMA 50 was above EMA 200 averaged +0.200R on 40
non-overlapping Databento candidates and +0.429R on seven HistData candidates.
This overlaps an earlier TradingView research hypothesis, but its Databento
chronological quarters averaged -0.2R, +1.0R, +0.6R, and -0.6R. The latest
quarter failure means the aggregate is not stable and must not be selected as
an edge from these data.

## Interpretation

Sequencing corrected the zero-candidate architecture, but it did not create an
edge. The useful result is that same-bar context was structurally impossible
and the causal replacement remains negative across Databento development
quarters. The 8-bar window, direction, and regime rules must not be tuned around
these outcomes.

Further edge research should focus on information not represented by the
current OHLCV proxy—session effects, economically defined volatility states,
and true futures trade/order-book imbalance—using a new pre-registered module.
Any candidate discovered from development data still requires the unseen
post-2026-08-07 holdout and frozen execution costs.
