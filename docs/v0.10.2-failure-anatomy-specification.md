# ITRF v0.10.2 Failure-Anatomy Specification

## Purpose

Version 0.10.2 diagnoses how the frozen v0.8 signal fails inside the validated
v0.10 event core. It does not change the entry signal, stop, target, holding
period, direction set, position sequencing, or cost assumptions. It does not
fit a model or search for a profitable filter.

This specification is frozen before the historical failure-anatomy report is
inspected.

## Frozen event universe

- XAU/USD 15-minute history already stored in the project
- The 212 accepted, one-position-at-a-time v0.10 events
- Entry at the next bar open
- Initial risk of 1.5 signal-bar ATR
- Stop at -1R, target at +3R, and timeout after 32 entry-inclusive bars
- Same-bar stop/target ambiguity retained as a separate event type
- No new download, live feed, broker connection, or order path

## Bar-level lifecycle panel

For every accepted event, store one row for each entry-to-exit bar with:

1. Event, source-bar, and holding-bar indices
2. Direction, entry price, and frozen risk distance
3. Favorable extreme in R for that OHLC bar
4. Adverse extreme in R for that OHLC bar
5. Running maximum favorable excursion (MFE) in R
6. Running maximum adverse excursion (MAE) in R
7. Terminal event on the exit bar

MFE is non-negative and MAE is non-positive, both measured directionally from
entry using the frozen initial risk.

## Pre-terminal excursion rule

OHLC data cannot establish whether an exit-bar extreme happened before or
after an intrabar stop or target. Therefore:

- stop, target, and ambiguous events exclude the terminal bar from
  `preterminal_mfe_r` and `preterminal_mae_r`
- timeout events include the terminal bar because the exit is the completed
  bar close
- events terminating on the entry bar have zero pre-terminal excursion
- full-path excursions including the terminal bar are retained separately and
  may not be interpreted as available before an exit decision

This rule prevents terminal-bar lookahead from overstating how much opportunity
existed before failure.

## Fixed excursion landmarks

The report may count whether pre-terminal MFE reached +0.5R, +1R, or +2R.
These are descriptive risk-unit landmarks fixed before results, not proposed
break-even, partial-exit, or trailing-stop rules. No other landmark may be
selected from the report.

## Competing event hazards

For holding bars 1 through 32, report:

- number at risk at the start of the bar
- stop, target, timeout, and ambiguous terminal counts
- cause-specific event count divided by the at-risk count
- discrete survival and cumulative incidence by cause

Pre-declared time phases are:

- entry phase: bars 1-4 (first hour)
- development phase: bars 5-16 (hours 1-4)
- late phase: bars 17-31
- forced close: bar 32

These phases are descriptive. They may not be converted into a holding-period
or time-of-entry rule on this inspected sample.

## Required integrity tests

Synthetic paths must prove:

1. Long and short favorable/adverse excursion signs are correct.
2. Stop/target terminal bars are excluded from pre-terminal excursion.
3. Timeout terminal bars are included.
4. Entry-bar terminal events have zero pre-terminal excursion.
5. At-risk counts and cause-specific hazards decrease consistently.
6. Invalid event intervals, risk, direction, and ledger/frame mismatches fail
   loudly.

## Interpretation rule

The report can reject a mechanism or motivate one future pre-registered
hypothesis. It cannot validate an edge, optimize trade management, select a
direction, or justify live/paper trading. Any proposed rule must be specified
later and evaluated on untouched chronological data.
