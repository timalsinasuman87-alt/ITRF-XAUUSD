# ITRF v0.10.5 Hazard-Management Specification

## Purpose

Version 0.10.5 tests one pre-declared use of the frozen v0.10.3 dynamic
competing-risk probabilities.  It asks whether a causal defensive exit can
improve the same accepted XAU/USD events relative to the unchanged v0.10
bracket.  It is not a threshold search, entry strategy, or profitability
claim.

The January 2025-April 2026 external segment has already been inspected for
v0.10.4 calibration.  This is therefore an **exploratory management-policy
screen**, not an untouched validation.  It may reject the policy or justify
freezing it for later validation, but it cannot promote the policy for use.

This specification is frozen before managed outcomes are calculated.

## Locked data and probability model

- Historical XAU/USD only
- Same locked development and external files and hashes as v0.10.4
- Fit the unchanged v0.10.3 dynamic model on May-August 2026 development risk
  rows only
- Apply it unchanged to January 2025-April 2026 external risk rows
- No external outcome enters fitting, preprocessing, or probability generation
- Keep the v0.8 entry, v0.10 event sequencing, 1.5 ATR stop distance, 3R
  target, and 32-bar maximum holding period unchanged
- Exclude same-bar stop/target ambiguous events from both policies

## One locked management action

For each accepted external event, retain the baseline entry and calculate the
dynamic model's next-bar `STOP` and `TARGET` probabilities at the start of each
at-risk bar.

Beginning with holding bar 2, close the complete position at that bar's open
when:

`prob_stop > 3 * prob_target`

This is equivalent to negative next-bar terminal pressure under the frozen
-1R stop and +3R target payoffs:

`3 * prob_target - prob_stop < 0`

The multiplier is derived from the locked payoff asymmetry, not selected from
historical results.  Bar 1 is excluded so the rule remains trade management
rather than an entry filter.  Use the earliest qualifying bar only.

If the condition never occurs, retain the original bracket/timeout outcome.
No stop is moved, no partial exit is taken, no target is changed, and no later
re-entry is added.  Signals rejected while the baseline position was open
remain rejected, even if the managed policy exits earlier.  This preserves a
one-to-one paired comparison and prevents extra opportunities from confounding
the management effect.

## Fill and cost assumptions

- Decision information: data completed through the prior bar only
- Managed fill: current bar open
- Managed gross R: directional open-to-entry movement divided by initial risk
- No same-bar close fill or intrabar look-ahead
- Round-trip cost sensitivities: 0.00R, 0.05R, and 0.10R per event

The cost cases are sensitivity deductions, not estimates of a specific
broker's spread, slippage, or commission.  Both paired policies receive the
same fixed round-trip deduction because each retains one entry and one exit.

## Locked aggregate metrics

- Eligible paired events
- Managed-action events and action frequency
- Mean and median action holding bar
- Average R for baseline and managed policies at each cost level
- Profit factor in R for both policies at each cost level
- Maximum sequential drawdown in R for both policies at each cost level
- Mean paired managed-minus-baseline R improvement
- Paired circular block-bootstrap 95% interval for mean improvement

No direction, year, lifecycle phase, probability band, event outcome, or other
subgroup may be selected after inspection.

## Decision rule

The hazard-management policy passes this exploratory screen only if all of the
following hold on the complete paired external sample:

1. mean managed-minus-baseline R is positive;
2. the paired 95% bootstrap interval has a lower bound above zero;
3. managed profit factor exceeds baseline profit factor at both 0.05R and
   0.10R round-trip cost; and
4. managed maximum drawdown is no worse than baseline at both 0.05R and 0.10R.

Failure on any condition rejects the policy.  A pass only permits the exact
unchanged rule to be reserved for a future chronological test.  It does not
establish a profitable strategy or authorize paper/live trading.

## Cost and safety

- Incremental data cost: $0
- No new download
- No live market or broker connection
- No order placement
- No futures trading
