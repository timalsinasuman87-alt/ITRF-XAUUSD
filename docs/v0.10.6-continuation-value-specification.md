# ITRF v0.10.6 Continuation-Value Specification

## Purpose

Version 0.10.6 tests whether a fixed continuation-value model can convert the
transportable v0.10.3 probability state into a more complete management
decision than the failed v0.10.5 one-bar hazard comparison.

For every post-entry at-risk state, the model estimates the expected advantage
in R of retaining the original stop/target/timeout path instead of exiting at
the current bar open.  This label includes all later `NONE`, `STOP`, `TARGET`,
and timeout outcomes observed under the unchanged baseline policy.

The January 2025-April 2026 external segment has already been inspected in
v0.10.4 and v0.10.5.  This is therefore an **exploratory decision-model
screen**, not untouched validation or a profitability claim.  The exact model,
features, action, and decision rule below are frozen before v0.10.6 managed
outcomes are calculated.

## Locked data and event architecture

- Historical XAU/USD only
- Same locked files, hashes, intervals, and independent segment construction
  as v0.10.4
- Unchanged v0.8 entries and v0.10 one-position candidate ledger
- Next-bar-open entry, 1.5 ATR initial risk, -1R stop, +3R target, and 32-bar
  maximum holding period
- Exclude same-bar stop/target ambiguous events
- Keep the same baseline event set under both policies
- Do not admit signals rejected during a baseline open position after an early
  managed exit

## Causal decision rows

Only holding bars 2-32 are eligible for a management action.  At the start of
holding bar *k*, the row may use:

- the frozen v0.10.3 time, pre-entry, and prior-completed-bar path features;
- the out-of-fold or externally transported dynamic probabilities for
  `NONE`, `STOP`, and `TARGET` during bar *k*; and
- the directional R available by exiting at bar *k* open (`exit_now_r`).

No current-bar high, low, close, terminal event, or later path value enters the
decision features.

The development training label is:

`continuation_advantage_r = baseline_terminal_gross_r - exit_now_r`

It therefore represents the complete realized value of all remaining baseline
states relative to exiting now, rather than only the next-bar terminal hazard.

## Leakage control and fixed model

Development hazard probabilities used to train the continuation model must be
the existing v0.10.3 purged expanding walk-forward predictions.  A development
row without an out-of-fold dynamic prediction is not used to train the value
model.  This prevents in-sample hazard estimates from becoming value features.

External hazard probabilities are produced exactly as in v0.10.4: fit the
frozen dynamic hazard model on development risk rows only and transport it
unchanged to the external rows.  External labels never enter either model.

The continuation model is one deterministic linear ridge regression with:

- the complete fixed decision feature set described above;
- development-only median imputation and weighted standardization;
- event-balanced row weights, so every development event has equal total
  fitting weight;
- unpenalized intercept;
- fixed L2 strength 1.0; and
- no feature selection, interaction search, nonlinear transform, clipping,
  or hyperparameter search.

## One locked management action

Beginning at holding bar 2, close the complete position at the earliest current
bar open where:

`predicted_continuation_advantage_r < 0`

Zero is the direct indifference boundary between continuing and exiting; it is
not selected from historical results.  If no eligible row is negative, retain
the original bracket/timeout outcome.  No partial exit, stop movement, target
change, re-entry, probability multiplier, or safety margin is permitted.

## Fill and cost treatment

- Decision uses information available at the current bar open
- Managed fill is the current bar open
- Managed gross R is directional open-to-entry movement divided by initial
  risk
- Both policies retain one entry and one exit
- Report fixed round-trip sensitivity deductions of 0.00R, 0.05R, and 0.10R
  per event

Because the paired policies have the same number of entries and exits, the
fixed completed-trade deduction cancels from their paired difference.  It still
affects average R, profit factor, and drawdown.  These values are sensitivities,
not estimates for a specific broker.

## Locked diagnostics and aggregate metrics

- Out-of-fold development training rows and events
- Eligible paired external events
- Managed-action count and frequency
- Mean and median action holding bar
- Baseline and managed average R at each cost level
- Baseline and managed profit factor at each cost level
- Baseline and managed maximum sequential drawdown at each cost level
- Mean paired managed-minus-baseline R
- Paired circular block-bootstrap 95% interval

No direction, year, lifecycle phase, predicted-value range, outcome, or other
subgroup may be selected after inspection.

## Decision rule

The continuation-value policy passes this exploratory screen only if:

1. mean managed-minus-baseline R is positive;
2. its paired 95% bootstrap interval has a lower bound above zero;
3. managed profit factor exceeds baseline profit factor at both 0.05R and
   0.10R cost; and
4. managed maximum drawdown is no worse than baseline at both 0.05R and 0.10R.

Failure on any condition rejects the policy.  A pass only permits the complete
unchanged model and action to be frozen for later chronological validation.  It
does not authorize paper or live trading.

## Cost and safety

- No live market or broker connection
- No order placement
- No futures trading
