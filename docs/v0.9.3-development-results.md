aggressor volume was summed from the source liquidity-sweep bar through the
confirmation bar, inclusive. Every bar required an exact UTC match. Longs

## Decision

Reject the simple sequence-delta sign gate as a strategic edge. It improved on
v0.9.2's confirmation-bar result but did not produce a positive margin before
realistic costs and was not chronologically stable. Do not select a direction,
delta magnitude, sequence age, or session from these outcomes.

TradingView's standard bar feed does not expose this true CME trade-aggressor
sequence. No Pine substitute will be presented as equivalent to the Python
definition.
