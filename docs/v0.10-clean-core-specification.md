# ITRF v0.10 Clean Research Core Specification

## Purpose

Version 0.10 is an architectural correction, not a new profitability claim or
parameter search. It replays the immutable v0.8 entry signal through a causal,
event-driven execution model so signal quality and execution assumptions can be
measured separately.

This specification is frozen before inspecting v0.10 replay results.

## Frozen benchmark

- Target instrument: XAU/USD
- Signal: unchanged `detect_setup` from v0.8
- Signal information time: completed 15-minute bar close
- Entry: next available bar open
- Initial risk distance: 1.5 times signal-bar ATR
- Stop: 1R from entry
- Target: 3R from entry
- Maximum holding period: 32 bars beginning with the entry bar
- Position rule: at most one open position across both directions
- Timeout: close of the final holding bar, converted to realized R
- Pyramiding: disabled
- Re-entry: a signal formed on an exit bar may enter on the following bar

No signal threshold, target, stop, holding period, direction, time window, or
regime may be selected from the replay results.

## Intrabar uncertainty

OHLC data cannot reveal whether a stop or target occurred first when both prices
fall inside one bar. Each such trade therefore records an outcome interval:

- Lower bound: stop first, -1R
- Upper bound: target first, +3R

Unambiguous trades have identical lower and upper outcomes. Aggregate results
must report both bounds. Neither bound is considered the true fill sequence.

## Cost handling

No broker cost is known, so the primary replay is gross. Fixed sensitivity
deductions of 0.00R, 0.05R, and 0.10R per completed trade may be reported. They
are diagnostics, not broker estimates.

## Event ledger

Every v0.8 candidate must be stored with one of these decisions:

- `ACCEPTED`
- `REJECTED_POSITION_OPEN`
- `REJECTED_NO_ENTRY_BAR`
- `REJECTED_INCOMPLETE_HORIZON`

Accepted trades store signal time, entry time, exit time, entry/stop/target,
risk, direction, bars held, exit reason, ambiguity flag, and lower/upper gross
R. Rejected candidates remain visible so sample compression cannot be hidden.
Historical candidates without all 32 future bars are right-censored and cannot
be accepted as completed trades.

## Validation requirements

Synthetic tests must prove:

1. Entry uses the next bar open, not the signal close.
2. Stop and target terminate the event immediately.
3. Same-bar stop/target produces the frozen lower/upper interval.
4. Timeout marks the final close in directional R.
5. One-position sequencing rejects overlapping candidates but permits a signal
   on the exit bar.
6. Invalid directions, ATR, and policy values are rejected.

## Interpretation rule

This replay can diagnose the frozen benchmark, but it cannot promote a strategy
because the data has already been inspected. A new edge hypothesis must be
pre-registered separately and later evaluated on untouched chronological data.
