# ITRF v0.10.2 Failure-Anatomy Results

## Status

The pre-registered v0.10.2 lifecycle diagnostic has been completed on the
already inspected XAU/USD development sample. It explains the frozen v0.8
signal's clean-core failure pattern; it does not test a new strategy, establish
profitability, or authorize an exit-rule change.

The specification, implementation, and synthetic integrity tests were
committed before the historical report was run. No new data was downloaded, no
live feed or order path was used, and the incremental cost was $0.

## Outcome path anatomy

Terminal-bar extremes are excluded from stop and target pre-terminal
excursions. Timeout excursions include bar 32 because timeout occurs at that
completed bar's close.

| Exit reason | Events | Median bars | Median pre-terminal MFE | Median pre-terminal MAE |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Stop | 155 | 6.0 | +0.526R | -0.735R |
| 3R target | 32 | 12.5 | +2.558R | -0.536R |
| 32-bar timeout | 25 | 32.0 | +1.679R | -0.503R |

The target population generally required more time than the stopped
population. The stop population's excursion distribution is broad: some
events failed almost immediately, while others first moved materially in the
favorable direction.

## Terminal timing

| Pre-declared phase | Stop | Target | Timeout | Total terminals |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Bars 1-4: entry phase | 59 | 6 | 0 | 65 |
| Bars 5-16: development | 66 | 14 | 0 | 80 |
| Bars 17-31: late | 28 | 12 | 0 | 40 |
| Bar 32: forced close | 2 | 0 | 25 | 27 |

Stops were 59 of the 65 entry-phase terminals (90.8%). By the end of bar 16,
125 of all 155 stops (80.6%) and 20 of all 32 targets (62.5%) had terminated.
This identifies early adverse selection as one failure mechanism, but these
inspected counts cannot justify a time filter or shorter holding period.

## Favorable movement before eventual stops

| Fixed pre-terminal MFE landmark | Stopped events | Share of 155 stops |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| At least +0.5R | 79 | 50.97% |
| At least +1.0R | 44 | 28.39% |
| At least +2.0R | 10 | 6.45% |

The complement is also important: 76 stopped trades (49.03%) never reached
+0.5R on a fully observed pre-terminal bar. At the same time, 44 stopped trades
had reached at least +1R before eventually stopping. The failure population is
therefore a mixture of immediate adverse selection and favorable-excursion
giveback, not one uniform mechanism.

These landmarks were frozen as diagnostics. They do not demonstrate that a
break-even stop, partial exit, or trailing stop would improve expectancy;
those rules can also truncate the target population and require a separate
causal replay with costs.

## Decision

Version 0.10.2 isolates two failure modes:

1. early adverse selection, concentrated in the first 16 holding bars
2. favorable-excursion giveback among a meaningful subset of eventual stops
   and timeouts

This is diagnosis, not an edge. No stop movement, partial profit, holding-time,
direction, or session rule is promoted from these inspected results.

## Next research increment

The next mathematical increment should be a pre-registered competing-risk
framework rather than another aggregate success classifier. It should estimate
separately, using only information known at each completed bar:

- near-term stop hazard
- target hazard
- continued-survival probability

The first version should be calibration-only and benchmarked against empirical
holding-bar hazards. No probability may be converted into a trade-management
action until the framework is frozen and evaluated on untouched chronological
data.
