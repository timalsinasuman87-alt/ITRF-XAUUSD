# ITRF v0.9.1 causal context hypothesis

## Research question

Does sequencing liquidity, location, structure, and displacement produce a
testable entry cohort where the v0.9 same-bar conjunction produced none?

This version changes context timing only. It does not change the preserved
v0.8 entry engine, forward outcome labels, risk distance, or exit models.

## Frozen causal definitions

The research timeframe remains 15 minutes.

1. A long setup is armed when a completed bar sweeps external sell-side
   liquidity and closes in discount. A short setup is armed symmetrically after
   a buy-side sweep in premium.
2. The sweep bar stores its low for long invalidation or high for short
   invalidation. The sweep bar cannot also confirm an entry.
3. Confirmation is allowed on bars 1 through 8 after arming. Eight bars equals
   two hours and is one quarter of the existing 32-bar outcome horizon. It was
   fixed before inspecting v0.9.1 outcomes and must not be tuned on this data.
4. Long confirmation requires a new bullish close-cross of the latest confirmed
   swing level (BOS or CHoCH) and bullish displacement on that later bar. Short
   confirmation is symmetric. Merely remaining beyond a swing is not a new
   structure event.
5. A close beyond the stored sweep extreme invalidates the active setup. An
   active setup also expires after bar 8.
6. A new qualifying sweep replaces or refreshes the active setup. Replacement
   takes precedence over confirmation on that bar, so every emitted signal is
   strictly later than its arming event.
7. A confirmation emits one candidate and immediately clears the state. Only
   one context state can be active at a time.

## Development and validation separation

All data through 2026-08-07 has already been inspected and is development data
for v0.9.1. It may be used for causal tests, candidate counts, data-quality
checks, and descriptive diagnostics, but not labelled as new OOS evidence.

The next true holdout begins after 2026-08-07. No v0.9.1 threshold may change
after holdout collection begins. A final edge decision requires at least 100
non-overlapping candidates and three calendar months of unseen data, whichever
takes longer, plus frozen broker-specific execution costs.

## Advancement criteria

- Python and Pine must emit the same event definitions on completed bars.
- Unit tests must prove no lookahead, no same-bar confirmation, expiry,
  invalidation, replacement, and one-signal state clearing.
- Development results must show enough candidates to make a future holdout
  feasible; candidate frequency alone is not an edge.
- A positive historical average or attractive chart is insufficient. Stability
  across time, direction, feed, execution costs, and independent holdout data is
  required before paper trading is considered.
