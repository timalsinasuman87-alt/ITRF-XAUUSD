# ITRF v0.10.7 Futures-Confirmation Results

## Status

The pre-registered v0.10.7 entry mechanism has been evaluated on the complete
locked development and backward-external intervals. A frozen v0.8 XAU/USD
candidate could enter only when the independently recorded, just-completed CME
Gold futures candle closed in the same direction. The sign boundary was zero;
no magnitude, direction, session, or other threshold was fitted.

The mechanism **failed** its pre-registered exploratory decision rule. It is
rejected and must not be rescued by selecting the older interval, changing the
sign rule, adding a body-size threshold, choosing one direction, or searching a
subgroup.

Both intervals were already inspected in earlier research. This is not
untouched validation or a profitability claim. No data was downloaded, no live
feed or broker connection was used, no order was placed, and the incremental
cost was $0. Historical futures supplied context only and were not traded.

## Pre-registered decision

The mechanism passed sample-size, total-return, drawdown, and implementation
integrity conditions. It failed the decisive expectancy and stability rules:

1. development cost-adjusted average R was negative;
2. the development confidence interval was wholly negative;
3. the backward-external confidence lower bound was not above zero;
4. development profit factor was below 1.0 at both locked costs; and
5. both development chronological halves were negative.

Failure on any one condition required rejection. No condition may be relaxed
after inspection.

## Research interpretation

The result is more informative than another price-only score. Same-bar CME
futures direction did separate outcomes in the older history, demonstrating
that an independent market can contain economically relevant context. But that
relationship reversed and became strongly adverse in the newer interval. It
is therefore a regime-dependent historical association, not a stable entry
edge under the frozen definition.

This also clarifies the next boundary: neither same-bar futures candle sign nor
previously tested futures aggressor-flow sign is sufficient by itself. The
project must not search candle magnitude, basis thresholds, lags, sessions, or
directions on these inspected intervals.

## Next research requirement

Quarantine the exact v0.10.7 sign gate. Preserve the v0.10 clean execution core
and the full candidate ledger. Before another strategy rule is tested, perform
a pre-registered cross-period invariance audit that diagnoses whether feed
alignment, contract rolls, or changing spot/futures price discovery can explain
the sign reversal without selecting a trading threshold.

Any later entry hypothesis must use a distinct, economically justified causal
state and must be frozen before a genuinely future XAU/USD holdout is observed.
No paper or live-trading discussion is justified by the positive older slice.
