# ITRF v0.10.7 Futures-Confirmation Specification

## Purpose

Version 0.10.7 tests one new causal entry mechanism rather than another exit
rule. The frozen v0.8 signal is formed from XAU/USD. It may enter only when the
independently recorded, just-completed CME Gold futures candle closes in the
same direction. The economic hypothesis is that agreement from exchange-traded
Gold price discovery can reject XAU/USD signals that are only feed-local price
and activity noise.

This is a model-free sign test. It does not add points to the v0.8 score, fit a
coefficient, select a magnitude, or optimize a threshold. The complete feature,
entry rule, sequencing, metrics, and decision rule below are frozen before any
gated outcome is calculated.

Both historical intervals have already been inspected in earlier research.
This is therefore an exploratory mechanism test, not untouched validation or a
profitability claim. Even a pass cannot authorize paper or live trading.

## Exact causal alignment

Join the completed XAU/USD signal bar and completed futures bar only when their
exact 15-minute UTC start timestamps match. Nearest-time joins, forward fills,
backward fills, and interpolation are prohibited.

Before any signal outcome was calculated, the timestamp audit found:

- Development: 5,997 exact matches among 6,466 XAU/USD bars
- Backward external: 28,808 exact matches among 31,330 XAU/USD bars
- Futures timestamps are unique and chronological
- Futures OHLCV fields are finite

A missing exact futures bar fails the gate. At a bar labelled *t*, both candles
are treated as complete at *t + 15 minutes*. The XAU/USD entry remains the next
bar open, so the confirmation uses no post-entry information.

## One frozen information feature and gate

For a v0.8 long signal:

`futures_confirmation = GC_close > GC_open`

For a v0.8 short signal:

`futures_confirmation = GC_close < GC_open`

A zero-body futures candle fails both directions. There is no minimum body,
ATR, volume, order-flow, session, basis, lag, or contract-roll threshold. The
gate uses no XAU/USD outcome and no future futures bar.

## Frozen XAU/USD execution

- Unchanged v0.8 signal definitions and parameters
- Signal information at completed XAU/USD bar close
- Entry at the following XAU/USD bar open
- Initial stop distance: 1.5 signal-bar ATR
- Target: +3R
- Stop: -1R
- Maximum holding period: 32 entry-inclusive bars
- Timeout marked to the final XAU/USD close
- Same-bar stop/target ambiguity preserved as lower and upper bounds
- Candidates without a complete future horizon remain right-censored

## Independent sequencing and candidate retention

Build two ledgers independently on each complete interval:

1. `baseline`: every frozen v0.8 candidate enters when no baseline position is
   open;
2. `futures_confirmed`: only candidates passing the exact sign gate may enter
   when no futures-confirmed position is open.

A signal rejected by the futures gate does not block a later gated signal.
This is required because an entry filter changes the position path. Comparing
only the already accepted baseline trades is prohibited.

Every raw v0.8 candidate remains in both ledgers with an explicit reason:
accepted, position open, missing futures bar, futures disagreement, zero-body
futures candle, incomplete horizon, or missing next entry bar.

## Locked metrics

Report development and backward external intervals separately:

- raw v0.8 candidates;
- exact-match, missing, agreeing, disagreeing, and zero-body counts;
- independently accepted baseline and gated trades;
- stop, target, timeout, and ambiguous counts;
- average R, total R, R profit factor, and maximum sequential drawdown at
  fixed completed-trade costs of 0.00R, 0.05R, and 0.10R;
- circular block-bootstrap 95% interval for gated average R after 0.05R cost;
  and
- average gated R after 0.05R cost in the first and second chronological halves.

The cost values are fixed sensitivities, not broker estimates. No direction,
month, session, volatility, body magnitude, roll proximity, or other subgroup
may become a decision rule after inspection.

## Pre-registered decision rule

The complete futures-confirmation mechanism passes only if all conditions hold:

1. at least 50 gated sequential trades occur in development and at least 300
   occur in the backward external interval;
2. gated average R after 0.05R cost is positive in both intervals;
3. the 95% interval for that average has a lower bound above zero in both
   intervals;
4. gated profit factor exceeds 1.0 after both 0.05R and 0.10R costs in both
   intervals;
5. gated total R exceeds the independently sequenced baseline total R after
   both 0.05R and 0.10R costs in both intervals;
6. gated maximum drawdown is no worse than baseline after both locked costs in
   both intervals;
7. first-half and second-half gated average R after 0.05R cost are each
   positive in both intervals; and
8. every data, timing, sequencing, ambiguity, and reproducibility check passes.

Failure on any condition rejects the mechanism. The result may not be rescued
by changing `>` to `>=`, adding magnitude, selecting one direction, changing
the interval, or choosing a favorable subgroup.
