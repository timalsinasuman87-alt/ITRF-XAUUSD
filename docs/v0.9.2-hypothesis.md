# ITRF v0.9.2 Pre-Registered Order-Flow Hypothesis

Status: pre-registered before downloading or inspecting the requested trade data.

## Purpose

v0.9.1 showed that a causal liquidity-sweep-to-structure-confirmation sequence
is not, by itself, a stable edge. v0.9.2 tests whether the same frozen sequence
is more informative when the confirmation bar is supported by actual CME Gold
futures trade aggressor flow.

This is a new-information test. It is not a search for more profitable
thresholds, and v0.8 and v0.9.1 remain unchanged.

## Frozen data definition

- Dataset: `GLBX.MDP3`
- Instrument: unadjusted front continuous Gold future, `GC.v.0`
- Input schema: Databento `trades`
- Research interval: 15-minute UTC bars, left-closed and left-labelled
- Buyer-initiated trade: Databento side `B`
- Seller-initiated trade: Databento side `A`
- Unspecified side: Databento side `N` or any unrecognised/missing value
- Buy volume: sum of trade size for side `B`
- Sell volume: sum of trade size for side `A`
- Volume delta: buy volume minus sell volume
- Signed-volume ratio: volume delta divided by buy plus sell volume
- Unspecified-side volume is reported but excluded from volume delta and its
  denominator.

All features use exchange events available by the close of their 15-minute
bar. No later trade may change an earlier bar.

## Primary hypothesis

Keep the v0.9.1 arm, invalidation, expiry, replacement, structure, and
displacement rules frozen. A v0.9.1 long confirmation passes the v0.9.2 flow
gate only when its completed confirmation bar has positive volume delta. A
short confirmation passes only when its completed confirmation bar has
negative volume delta.

Zero delta, missing flow, or a bar whose aggressor-side coverage is zero does
not pass either direction. No minimum magnitude will be selected from the
development results.

The futures flow bar joins to the XAU/USD confirmation bar by the exact UTC
15-minute bar-start timestamp. A missing exact match fails the gate; nearest
timestamp matching is prohibited.

The frozen 32-bar forward outcome remains unchanged. Non-overlap is calculated
separately for (a) all v0.9.1 confirmations in the common data interval and
(b) only confirmations accepted by the v0.9.2 flow gate. This prevents a
rejected event from unnecessarily blocking a later accepted event while still
providing a like-period v0.9.1 comparator.

## Diagnostics that are not promotion rules

Trade count, total volume, unknown-side share, signed-volume ratio, session,
and contract-roll proximity may be reported to explain data quality and
failure modes. They must not silently become entry filters. Any later use
requires a separately pre-registered version.

## Evaluation discipline

- The May 1 through August 7, 2026 sample is development data.
- Results from this sample cannot establish profitability.
- Report long and short separately, chronological stability, sample size,
  stop frequency, average R, and cost sensitivity.
- Cost sensitivity subtracts fixed round-trip burdens of 0.05R and 0.10R from
  every accepted outcome. These are diagnostics, not calibrated broker costs.
- Do not tune the sign rule, confirmation window, entry score, risk multiple,
  or exit model after seeing these results.
- Promotion requires a later untouched holdout after August 7, 2026 with at
  least 100 non-overlapping observations and at least three calendar months,
  plus frozen realistic costs.
