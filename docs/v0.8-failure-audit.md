# ITRF v0.8 Frozen Failure Audit

## Decision

The v0.8 baseline is preserved unchanged. On the available historical XAU/USD
sample it failed to demonstrate a stable, executable edge. This is a failure of
evidence, not proof that its true expectancy is exactly the observed value.

No v0.8 threshold was optimized, no direction or time window was selected, and
no live data or trading connection was used. The audit cost was $0 because it
used the already stored historical data.

## Data and reproducibility

The frozen Python definitions were replayed on 6,466 XAU/USD 15-minute bars
from 2026-05-01 00:00 through 2026-08-07 16:45. The source is HistData midpoint
bid/ask OHLC. `volume` is tick count, an activity proxy rather than traded
volume. Source timestamps are preserved as supplied and are not assumed to be
UTC or an exchange session.

The audit reproduced 384 v0.8 observations, matching the prior checkpoint:

- 199 observations before the existing 2026-06-20 split
- 185 observations after it
- -0.180R gross average over all 384 observations

Only 107 observations remained after a mechanical one-position-at-a-time rule
that suppresses new entries for the frozen 32-bar forward window. Therefore,
277 of 384 observations (72.1%) represented overlapping or clustered exposure,
not separate sequential trades.

## What failed

### 1. The entry evidence did not produce positive expectancy

| Population | Samples | Average R | Block-bootstrap 95% interval | R profit factor | Maximum drawdown |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| All v0.8 observations | 384 | -0.180 | -0.349 to -0.003 | 0.753 | 75R |
| Non-overlapping observations | 107 | -0.196 | -0.430 to +0.037 | 0.744 | 25R |

The all-observation interval is not a valid independence claim because signals
overlap. The sequential interval crosses zero, but it does not support a
positive edge. Any spread, slippage, or commission would lower the result.

### 2. Adverse movement dominated the frozen payoff label

For the 107 non-overlapping observations:

- 82 were labelled -1R, 5 were +1R, 4 were +2R, and 16 were +3R
- 76.6% encountered the 1.5 ATR stop
- 51.4% touched +1R
- 28.0% touched +2R
- 17.8% touched +3R
- median MFE was 1.164R and median MAE was 1.140R

The setups often moved in both directions. The entry did not separate favorable
from adverse paths strongly enough for the frozen stop/horizon definition.

### 3. The forward evaluator contains an outcome-accounting defect

The frozen function records target touches but does not exit at +1R, +2R, or
even +3R. It continues through the 32-bar path, and any later stop overwrites a
previous target as -1R. Across all 384 observations, this occurred after a
recorded:

- +1R touch 85 times
- +2R touch 24 times
- +3R touch 6 times

Six paths also contained a stop and target in the same 15-minute bar, where the
intrabar order is unknowable from OHLC alone. These ambiguities were handled
conservatively, which is defensible, but they must be isolated rather than
silently mixed with known event order.

A diagnostic stop-versus-1R first-touch label averaged -0.031R over all signals
and +0.028R over the 107 non-overlapping observations. That alternative is not
promoted: it was inspected after the fact, excludes cost, and is too close to
zero to establish an edge. It shows that part of the reported v0.8 loss came
from outcome accounting, while the entry itself still lacks demonstrated
predictive strength.

## Where it failed

### Direction

| Direction | Sequential samples | Average R | 95% interval | Stop rate | R profit factor |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Long | 42 | -0.381 | -0.810 to +0.095 | 83.3% | 0.543 |
| Short | 65 | -0.077 | -0.385 to +0.215 | 72.3% | 0.894 |

Long observations were the larger weakness, but neither direction has a
confidence interval that establishes positive expectancy. The result must not
be used to select shorts only.

### Supposed setup strength

| Group | Sequential samples | Average R | R profit factor |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Sweep present | 58 | -0.190 | 0.756 |
| No sweep | 49 | -0.204 | 0.730 |
| Score 4 | 91 | -0.187 | 0.757 |
| Score 5 | 16 | -0.250 | 0.667 |

The weighted score was not monotonic: score 5 did not outperform score 4.
Liquidity sweeps also did not separate outcomes. This undermines the score's
interpretation as increasing evidence quality.

### Volatility and clock windows

High- and low-volatility groups were both negative. The 06-11 source-time bin
was weak and the 18-23 bin was flat, but the source timezone is not established
as an exchange session and the bins were inspected after the fact. They are
failure diagnostics, not filters to add.

## When it failed

The non-overlapping chronological quarters averaged:

| Development quarter | Samples | Average R |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Q1 | 24 | +0.083 |
| Q2 | 23 | -0.478 |
| Q3 | 33 | -0.242 |
| Q4 | 27 | -0.148 |

Only the first quarter was slightly positive; the next three were negative.
The edge therefore did not remain stable through the sample. This is more
important than any isolated profitable slice.

## Why it failed

The evidence supports several mechanisms rather than one magical bad setting:

1. **The score is not a calibrated probability model.** It adds hand-assigned
   integer points and assumes that more points mean stronger evidence, which
   the observed score groups did not confirm.
2. **The components are not independent.** Momentum, trend, sweep, close
   location, and the delta proxy are all transformations of the same OHLC path.
   Relative volume and delta also reuse the same current-bar tick activity. The
   score can count one price impulse several times without adding new
   information.
3. **The delta input is not order flow.** `delta_proxy` is candle close location
   multiplied by tick count. It cannot identify aggressor buying and selling.
   Later v0.9.2 and v0.9.3 tests of actual historical futures aggressor-flow
   sign also failed their pre-registered development tests, so replacing the
   proxy with a simple sign gate was not enough.
4. **Different setup families share one label.** A sweep is worth two points,
   so sweep setups need only two other conditions, while non-sweep setups need
   all four other conditions. These are structurally different hypotheses but
   were pooled as one strategy.
5. **Signals cluster.** Treating all 384 rows as trades exaggerates sample size
   and repeatedly measures the same market episode.
6. **Outcome and execution were conflated.** The forward label is useful for
   path research but is not an executable exit model. The target-overwrite
   defect made that mismatch worse.
7. **The evidence base is narrow.** It covers roughly three months, one
   historical spot-data source, no broker-specific spread/slippage, and an
   activity proxy rather than broker volume. It is insufficient for a robust
   quant-level claim.

## Requirements for the cleaner successor

The next engine must be built as a research architecture, not as another score
with extra indicators:

1. Keep v0.8 immutable as the benchmark.
2. Separate signal generation, event ordering, execution simulation, cost,
   sizing, and reporting into testable modules.
3. Use a causal state machine: context forms first, a sweep arms one setup, a
   later confirmed structural event may trigger it, and the state expires or
   invalidates deterministically.
4. Treat market regime, structure, liquidity, location, and confirmation as
   explicit hypotheses with causal timestamps—not interchangeable points.
5. Store all eligible events, rejected events, reason codes, first-event times,
   MFE/MAE, gaps, and intrabar ambiguity.
6. Enforce one-position-at-a-time evaluation and report raw candidates
   separately from sequential trades.
7. Use executable exit rules that terminate when an order fills, with explicit
   same-bar policy and cost sensitivity.
8. Pre-register one hypothesis at a time, freeze its definitions, and test it
   on development data before acquiring untouched chronological validation.
9. Require stability across direction, regime, and time; do not rescue a failed
   hypothesis by selecting a profitable slice.
10. Acquire longer independent XAU/USD history before making a promotion
    decision. Historical futures data may validate context, but XAU/USD remains
    the traded research target and no live feed is authorized.

## Reproduction

From the repository with its environment activated:

```bash
python research/audit_v08_failure.py
python -m pytest -q
```

The audit writes its detailed local observations to
`data/processed/v08_failure_audit.csv`. The generated CSV is ignored by Git;
the audit code, tests, and this evidence record are versioned.
